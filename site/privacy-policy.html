By installing and using Expense Counter you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Contact: tusharvarshney1810@gmail.com Published at: /privacy-policy

1. Summary

  • We collect the minimum data needed to run the app: your account details, the expenses you record, shared group records, the messages and reports you send, and a device push token when notifications are enabled. Your monthly budget is stored on your device only.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads and we do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, or health data.
  • You can delete your entire account and all associated data from inside the app at any time (Settings → Delete account).

3. How we use your data

We use your data only to provide the app:

  1. Authentication — to create and secure your account.
  2. Expense tracking — to display, total and store the expenses you add.
  3. Chat — to deliver messages between you and recipients.
  4. Notifications — to alert you to new chat requests when notifications are enabled.
  5. Trust & safety — to review reports of abuse and enforce our Terms.

Shared group data is used to calculate each member's balance and to show settle-up suggestions. A settlement is only a record of a payment members report; the app does not transfer money.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app for you.

4. Legal basis

Where required by law we process your data on the basis of performance of a contract (providing the app you signed up for). Optional features such as receipt photos and notifications rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through your device settings or by simply not using the feature.

5. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database and Supabase Storage.
  • For offline use, the app also keeps expenses and recent messages in its local SQLite database, and stores your monthly budget in app-local preferences. The app does not enable SQLCipher for this cache; it relies on Android/iOS app sandboxing and the device's own security. Sign-out and account deletion clear this cached account data and preference. Use a device screen lock and do not share an unlocked device.
  • All traffic between the app and the servers is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Data is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider.
  • Row Level Security is enabled on every table: personal expense rows are readable only by their owner, shared group records only by group members, and messages only by their two participants. UPI IDs are available only to the account owner and members of their shared groups. Messages between a blocked user and you are hidden in both directions.
  • Message recipients can review, accept, or decline requests. You can block a sender or report a user; blocking declines that sender's pending request. Reports are restricted to the app's review process and cannot be read by ordinary users.
  • Your session is stored on-device in the platform secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • Receipt photos are held in a private storage bucket; each user can access only their own folder.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

7. Camera, photos and biometrics

  • Camera / Photos: requested only when you choose to attach a receipt. Images are used solely for that receipt and are never captured in the background.
  • Biometrics: the optional app lock uses Face ID / fingerprint / device passcode. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

8. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Individual expenses can be deleted at any time; messages you send are removed together with your account. When you delete your account, the data described in section 9 is removed from the app and server, including receipt photos. Email requests are processed within 30 days.

9. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account from inside the app:

Settings → Delete account → confirm.

If you cannot access the app, submit a request at /account-deletion or email tusharvarshney1810@gmail.com from your registered email address. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your profile (email, display name, avatar);
  • all of your personal expenses and their receipt images;
  • all messages you have sent;
  • your push token;
  • shared expenses and settlements involving your account;
  • the local database stored on your device.

If other members remain, the group continues and another member becomes its admin. If you were the only member, the group is deleted. After deletion you are signed out and the data cannot be recovered. If you cannot access the app, email tusharvarshney1810@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete the account within 30 days.

10. Children

Expense Counter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers
