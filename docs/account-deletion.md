# Delete your Expense Counter account

This page is for anyone who wants to request account and data deletion without
opening the app.

## Delete from the app

If you can sign in, open **Settings → Delete account** and confirm. This removes
your account and associated cloud data, receipt photos, and cached app data from
the device.

## Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email **tusharvarshney1810@gmail.com** from the
email address registered to your Expense Counter account. Use the subject
**Expense Counter account deletion**. If you cannot send from that address,
include the account email and explain why; we may ask for enough information to
verify the request. Do not send your password or payment information.

We will process a verified request within 30 days. We may retain information
where the law requires it, and will explain any exception in our response.

## Data removed

Deletion removes the profile, personal expenses, shared group records associated
with the account, messages, blocks, reports, push token, and receipt photos.
Local cached expenses, messages, and the account's monthly budget preference are
cleared from the app. The app does not
transfer money, and deleting an account does not reverse any payment made
outside the app.
